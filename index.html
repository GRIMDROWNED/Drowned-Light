Drowned Light

Will you thrive in the light or drown in the darkness?

By day venture into the wetland and gather supplies. The farther from camp, the richer the land — and the deadlier the Drowned.
By night stay in the light. Darkness corrupts you; at full corruption you become a Drowned Knight.
At camp deposit supplies, build defenses, train your kit, and defend the Central Flame through the night.
