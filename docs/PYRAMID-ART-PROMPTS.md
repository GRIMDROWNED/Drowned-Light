# Pyramid art prompts

Draft prompts for Robert to generate the art listed in [PYRAMID-PLAN.md](PYRAMID-PLAN.md).
No art has been generated by this document, and it changes no gameplay. Names and sizes below
are **proposed production targets**, not registered assets or requirements on Robert's source images.
Robert decides the final designs. Claude owns any renderer or cutter changes needed to use them.

## Shared direction

Use the existing `assets/sprites/props/flame1.png`–`flame3.png`, `forge1.png`–`forge4.png`,
`brazier.png`, `palisade.png`, `gatePost.png`, and the `ground/` textures as visual references.
The marsh is dark, wet and old: moss-grey stone, charcoal timber, tarnished bronze, black iron,
worn cloth and amber firelight. Reserve cyan for the distant Drowned influence. The pyramid is
a refuge the knights built from that world, with careful repairs and signs of use.

Append this direction to every **object** prompt:

> A production game sprite for Drowned Light, detailed dark-fantasy pixel art matching the
> supplied game references. Fixed orthographic three-quarter overhead camera, looking north
> across the marsh; screen down is south. No isometric diamond, vanishing-point perspective,
> camera roll or camera change. Crisp pixel clusters, readable dark outline, controlled small
> highlights, weathered materials and a clear silhouette when reduced to game size. Soft neutral
> light comes from screen upper left in every image. One complete isolated asset, fully inside
> its canvas with generous blank margin. Uniform flat pure magenta background, RGB 255, 0, 255,
> no gradient, floor plane, cast shadow, vignette or background scenery. No letters, numbers,
> labels, UI, watermark, people or weapons held by a character.

Keep the camera and global light fixed when changing an asset's direction; do not rotate the
finished picture or mirror its highlights. The returned source can be a large PNG; the cutter
sets the final pixel scale. If an object's intended colours include magenta or purple, use a
flat **pure green** source background instead and record that choice for the cutter.

For buildings and markers, place the contact point at the horizontal centre of the base. Keep
this point identical between levels. For edge strips, use an explicit repeat seam and placement
edge instead of the usual bottom-centre prop anchor. Avoid glows that cross a repeat seam.

## 1. Raised stone faces and top lip

Generate each direction separately. These are modular stone strips, **not pictures of the whole
800 px square**. Matching mortar courses and edge colours must repeat without an obvious join.

Base prompt, followed by one direction from the table:

> A seamless section of the retaining stonework of a stepped refuge pyramid, built from large
> worn grey-brown marsh stones with narrow dark mortar joints, a few restrained moss patches and
> chipped corners. Sturdy and assembled by knights, not an Egyptian monument. Regular stone
> courses run straight along the step; alternate short and long blocks without a unique central
> symbol. The top lip catches a narrow pale highlight, and the foot is slightly damp and darker.
> No stairs, torches, plants extending over the edge, soil mound or scenery. Both repeat ends
> have exactly matching stone-course heights, brightness and silhouette. Show only the masonry
> strip on the flat key background. Follow the shared camera, palette and light direction.

| Proposed source | Direction addition | Proposed cut target / seam |
|---|---|---|
| `pyramid/step-south.png` | South edge, facing the viewer: the vertical wall face is visible, with a narrow top ledge. | 128 × 18 file px per one-level course; repeat left/right. |
| `pyramid/step-north.png` | North edge, facing away: show its upper stone lip and the thin visible far-edge masonry; preserve the same overhead camera. | 128 × 18 reference strip; repeat left/right, with most depth visually subdued. |
| `pyramid/step-east.png` | East edge, facing screen right: the long course runs vertically up/down the image; only its narrow right face is visible from the fixed camera. | 18 × 128 reference strip; repeat top/bottom. |
| `pyramid/step-west.png` | West edge, facing screen left: the long course runs vertically up/down the image; preserve the fixed light rather than mirroring the east asset. | 18 × 128 reference strip; repeat top/bottom. |

For sources, a 1024 × 256 canvas is useful for horizontal strips; transpose it for vertical
strips. Target sizes are guides: the current coded renderer shows broad south faces and thinner
lips on the other edges, so the final four directions need not occupy equal screen depth.

Separate top-lip prompt:

> A straight tileable coping strip for the same weathered marsh-stone pyramid. Show the narrow
> top surface and its chipped front edge, a restrained upper-left highlight and dark lower seam.
> No wall face below it. Uniform thickness, no ends or terminal posts; left and right repeat
> seamlessly. Isolated on pure magenta. Match the stone faces exactly in palette and block scale.

Proposed `pyramid/step-lip.png`: 1024 × 128 source; 128 × 8 file px cut. A vertical version can be
generated against the approved reference if required; do not assume a rotated light direction.
Extra level drops repeat stone courses; a full multi-level wall should not be baked into one sprite.

### East and west stairs, redo (added by Claude, 2026-11-03)

> left, isolated on pure magenta, no ground or shadow.

For the west stair use the same prompt with "toward the LEFT side (west)" and the landing at the RIGHT edge;
keep the light from the upper left rather than mirroring.

## 4. Barracks, three levels

Render one level per image, using the approved first image as the reference for the next two.
The barracks makes champions. These are buildings only; champion unit art is a separate pass.

Common prompt:

> The knights' barracks on a raised stone square in the Drowned Light refuge, a compact
> defensible building of damp marsh stone, blackened timber and dark iron. Front entrance
> faces screen south, roof and a little of the right side visible from the game's fixed
> three-quarter overhead camera. No sprawling courtyard or separate outbuildings. A plain
> weapon rack beside the open entrance suggests a place where champions muster, but contains
> no person. A small empty lantern hook, unlit lantern, and restrained faded ember-brown cloth
> tie it to the camp. Weathered, repaired and believable, with a readable stepped silhouette.
> No lettering, heraldic text, fire, smoke, glows or baked ground shadow. Isolated on pure
> magenta, shared upper-left light. Ground contact centred at the bottom of the building.

Append one level description:

1. **Timber barracks:** low stone foundation, rough timber walls, simple dark shingle roof,
   reinforced door and modest weapon rack. Practical shelter assembled from salvaged materials.
2. **Stone barracks:** same footprint and entrance, fuller stone walls, stronger iron-braced
   door, repaired roof ridge, a narrow loft window and short side buttresses. Clearly the first
   building upgraded, not a differently oriented replacement.
3. **Champion hall:** same footprint and entrance, heavier fitted stone, a raised central roof
   and two compact battlements, bronze corner caps and a pair of plain dark banners. A strong
   final silhouette without becoming a castle larger than the square.

Proposed sources `pyramid/barracks-1.png`–`barracks-3.png`: 1024 × 1024 each. Proposed final
widths 140 / 156 / 172 file px, with the same ground anchor and entrance position across levels.
Use the existing Forge's approximately 124 px width and knight's roughly 80 px height as scale
references. Keep growth chiefly upward; do not let roof eaves intrude on the square's stairs.

## 6. Finite-map rim: deep water and Abyss shore

Ground textures differ from object sprites: they are **opaque RGB tiles**, with no chroma key
or transparent background. The existing `GROUND` cutter downsamples to 200 × 200 and blends
eight texels off the repeat edges, currently yielding 192 × 192 tiles. One ground texel covers
two world px. Preserve that workflow unless Claude changes it.

## 7. Barracks champion (character, added by Claude)

The champions the barracks trains still borrow the Lantern Knight's sprites with a gold ring under
them. They need their own character, made the same way as every other knight and enemy: **two
6-pose sheets (3 × 2) plus a 4-frame front walk and a 4-frame back walk**, magenta background. Attach
the Lantern Knight image (`kit-refs/knight-front.png`) as the style and scale reference.

Design: a **Pyramid Champion**, the refuge's sworn guard. Heavier and broader than a player knight,
in tarnished bronze plate over dark chainmail, with a closed great-helm with a thin vertical visor
slit and a short ember-orange plume. A tall rectangular tower shield of riveted dark timber, bound in
bronze, with a simple stepped-pyramid emblem in pale stone (a shape, not letters). A short heavy
war-axe. Faded ember-brown tabard. It must read as an ally at a glance: warm bronze and ember
colours, no cyan, no Drowned rot.

Sheet A prompt:

> A pixel-art character sprite sheet for Drowned Light, detailed dark-fantasy pixel art matching the
> attached knight reference in style, outline and scale. A Pyramid Champion: a broad, heavily armoured
> guard in tarnished bronze plate over dark chainmail, closed great-helm with a thin vertical visor
> slit and a short ember-orange plume, a tall rectangular riveted timber tower shield bound in bronze
> with a pale stone stepped-pyramid emblem, a short heavy war-axe, a faded ember-brown tabard. Six
> poses in a 3 × 2 grid, each in its own cell with blank margin, the same size and feet on the same
> line: 1 standing facing the viewer, 2 standing seen from behind, 3 standing facing right in side
> view, 4 to 6 walking facing right in side view, legs in sharply different positions (front leg far
> forward, legs passing, back leg far behind). Fixed three-quarter overhead camera, light from the
> upper left. Uniform flat pure magenta background RGB 255, 0, 255, no ground, no shadow, no text.

Sheet B prompt: the same character and grid, poses: 1 shield raised and axe drawn back (attack

Walk sheets: "The same Pyramid Champion walking **toward the viewer** / **away from the viewer**,
four frames in one row, four sharply different leg positions, same size and feet on the same line,
pure magenta background."

Proposed folder `returned/pyramid-champion/`, cut like the other characters (a `SETS` entry in
`tools/cut_ai_sprites.py`). The game shows it at 0.85 × knight scale (`DL.PYRAMID.champion.scale`).

## 8. The cross of stairways (lane tile, added by Claude, 2026-11-03)

Robert's layout splits the pyramid into four quarters by a cross of long stairways that run from the
camp's four gates down to the marsh (v0.15.189). The game currently draws those lanes as coded grey
paving. This prompt makes one repeating stretch of that stairway. The drawn flights at each drop stay
as they are. The tile is used for all four arms: as drawn for north and south, and turned on its side
for east and west.

> A seamless vertical section of a long, shallow stone stairway seen from almost straight above, for a
> dark-fantasy pixel-art game, in the same style, stone colours and pixel size as the attached stair
> and stone-face references. A paved processional stair about four knights wide, made of many low,
> broad, worn grey-brown stone treads running straight across the strip, each tread a thin band with
> a narrow dark shadow line at its front edge, a few cracked or chipped treads, a little moss and damp
> in the joints. A low, dark kerb of squared stones runs along both long edges of the stair. Seen from
> overhead, so no risers, side walls or height are visible, only the tops of the treads and the kerbs.
> The strip runs from the very top edge of the image to the very bottom edge, and the top and bottom
> ends match exactly so it tiles endlessly with no visible join. No landing, no torches, no plants or
> objects over the stairs, no symbol in the middle. The strip is centred and fills about one quarter
> of the image's width. Light from the upper left. Uniform flat pure magenta background RGB 255, 0,
> 255 on both sides of the strip, no ground, no shadow, no text.

Use a tall portrait canvas (1024 × 1536 works). Attach `assets/sprites/pyramid/stair-south.png` and
`step-south.png` as style references.

Proposed source `returned/pyramid/stairway-lane.png`. It would be cut by `tools/cut_pyramid.py` to
about 130 world px wide (the lane is `stairW` 110 plus its kerbs) and tiled along the lane in
`lane()` in `src/pyramid.js`. Until it arrives, the coded paving stays.

## Delivery and art-pass checks

- Keep the original returned PNGs and their prompt/reference notes in the project art sources.
  Those source sheets are normally outside this repository at `art-prompts/returned`.
- Cut through `tools/cut_ai_sprites.py` / `tools/cutlib.py`; add deterministic source tables,
  key colours, target scales and anchors there. Do not hand-edit the exported PNGs.
- Props currently use `res: 2`; two file pixels represent one art pixel. Keep file pixels,
  logical art pixels and world pixels distinct when recording sizes. Ground tiles follow the
  separate sampling rule above.
- Check one sample in the game at normal scale before generating all levels. Check faces and
  lips repeated at least four times, staircase openings, consistent mortar courses, unchanged
  anchors and an unlit brazier under the live fire overlay.
- Register approved outputs in `src/sprites.js` or the approved ground/step loader and in the
  `sw.js` cache. Use `DL.asset` for loads and update matching release version stamps when shipped.
  These proposed filenames are not currently registered by this document.
- No maps, collision rules, costs, save fields, character stats or quest unlocks change as part
  of writing or generating these prompts. Art integration is a later implementation pass.
